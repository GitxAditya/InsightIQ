## Project Overview

**InsightIQ** is an interactive Excel-based Business Intelligence solution designed to simulate how an organization could monitor sales, customers, products, finance, inventory, and business performance from a centralized analytical system.

The project goes beyond simply creating charts.

It follows an end-to-end analytics workflow:

```text
Raw Data
   ↓
Data Cleaning
   ↓
Data Transformation
   ↓
Data Modeling
   ↓
Calculated Columns & DAX Measures
   ↓
PivotTables & Analytical Views
   ↓
Interactive Dashboards
   ↓
Business Insights
   ↓
Executive Decision Support
